# R014-A: Cross-head covariance and robust flip-rate calibration
Parent SHA: 5f0d21fc49c7b9f9ebd74c038ac7555bf593d23d. Two active nodes QKV-A / QKV-A-signal-noise-identifiability. CPU mathematical evidence; not pretrained model or approved architecture.

**QKV-A exact threshold.** Freeze a scalar projected downstream readout and two zero-mean head error variables e1,e2. Original total MSE is Var(e1)+Var(e2)+2Cov(e1,e2); perfectly removing head1 leaves Var(e2). Hence a locally beneficial intervention *harms total error* exactly when Cov(e1,e2)<-Var(e1)/2. For unit marginal variances the condition is rho<-0.5. With rho=-.8, theory total MSE increases .4 ->1.0; in 120,000 seeded Gaussian CPU pairs it increased .396829 ->1.003350, even as first head error vanished. This extends R013's perfect cancellation to an open covariance regime; it is textbook covariance algebra, not a novel QKV method. It holds at the frozen projected readout only: downstream autoregressive dynamics may differ.

Strongest prior/equivalent controls: WitCert arXiv:2607.28699v3 already studies cross-layer cancellation; ordinary GLS and multivariate ridge handle covariance; OptR arXiv:2608.02691 directly optimizes post-W_O attention error. Native discriminating task: on Qwen3-0.6B pinned activation slices measure per-Q-head projected error covariance (including V,W_O, residual), aggregate hidden/next-token KL, calibration-fitted ordinary global correction and label-matched GLM/GLS, no correction, and post-output oracle only as privileged upper bound. Falsifier: harmful covariance regime absent, or ordinary coupled controls resolve actual task. Need full model/data/runtime pin and separate Work review. Stage analytic advance / native incomplete.

**QKV-A child robust sign calibration.** Under the prior constructed sign-flip model where within-domain labels identify effective b=sF, F=-1 with probability rho, but rho is only known to lie in an EXTERNALLY justified interval [rho_lo,rho_hi], define predicted true sign action kappa*b with kappa>=0. Expected excess loss is R(kappa,rho)=2g²(1+kappa²-2kappa(1-2rho)). Its worst case is rho_hi, giving classic minimax kappa*=max(0,1-2rho_hi). If rho_hi>=.5, safely abstain from sign correction. With g=.25 and interval [.30,.45], nominal kappa=.4 yields regret .105 at rho .30 but .135 at rho .45; robust kappa=.1 yields .11625/.12375; no-label .125. No estimator can obtain the uncertainty interval for free from an observationally confounded single domain. Strongest baselines are ordinary correctly specified hierarchical Bayes, likelihood calibration, no-label and ridge with equal *trusted* anchor sample counts. Falsifier: interval unidentifiable or ordinary robust Bayes wins. No actual Qwen supervised target/labels.
