# R005-A — Calibration can reverse correlation-cancellation gains
Parent: QKV-A; R004. Status: advanced, deep mathematical record; no accepted architecture.
## Operator, information and objective
y1=theta+z, y2=theta+(1+b)z+u, independent z~N(0,1), u~N(0,tau²), tau>0. D=y2-y1=bz+u, v=b²+tau². Estimate theta_hat=y1-beta_hat D, with beta_hat determined by independent calibration, not the current test noise.
R(beta)=1-2b beta+v beta²=R*+v(beta-beta*)², beta*=b/v, R*=tau²/v. Expected risk exceeds ignoring y2 (risk 1) iff E[(beta_hat-beta*)²]>b²/v².
## Consequential finite-sample derivation
Labeled calibration supplies n independent triples (y1,y2,theta); z is therefore observable there, not at test. No-intercept OLS beta_hat=sum(Dz)/sum(D²).
Gaussian regression gives z=beta*D+eta with eta independent of D, Var(eta)=R*. Thus conditional coefficient variance is R*/sum D².
sum D²/v~chi²_n; integrating its density yields E[1/chi²_n]=Gamma(n/2-1)/(2 Gamma(n/2))=1/(n-2), n>2.
Therefore E R(beta_hat)=[tau²/(b²+tau²)](n-1)/(n-2), and for b!=0 this beats risk 1 iff n>2+tau²/b².
Examples: b=tau=1 gives risk 1 at n=3, 3/4 at n=4. b=1/4,tau=1 gives 24/17 at n=4, 1 at n=18, 288/289 at n=19.
For n<=2 unregularized expected risk diverges under tau>0, although individual sampled estimates are almost surely finite. b=0 means any estimation variation harms. tau=0,b!=0 is degenerate noiseless regression and excluded from divergence statement. Estimating an intercept changes degrees of freedom.
## Identifiability and simple repair
Difference-only calibration observes N(0,b²+tau²), identical for ±b. Averaging the two worlds' risk gives 1+v E[beta_hat²]>=1: no guaranteed gain in both. This is NOT an impossibility for all unlabeled calibration: full pairs with theta independent of noise reveal Cov(y1,D)=b.
Shrinking beta_hat by lambda in [0,1] gives E R=R*+(1-lambda)² b²/v+lambda² R*/(n-2). Oracle lambda*=b²/[b²+tau²/(n-2)]. This is known regression bias–variance control; independent-validation shrinkage/ridge are simple controls. R004 ordinary two-head construction already expresses correction.
