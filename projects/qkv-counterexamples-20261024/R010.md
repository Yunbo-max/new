## Most important correction to prior rounds

**R009 A-child interpretation narrowed.** With g=0.25, T=9, known correct Gaussian prior and label-noise variance 1, n=4 labels:

- Zero-label midpoint: excess MSE = **0.12500**.
- Hard sign decision: excess MSE = **0.147874**.
- Bayes posterior-mean decision: excess MSE = **0.094651**.

Thus 'four labels hurt' is not an intrinsic limit; it is an overconfident decision baseline. Standard Bayes shrinkage should be included before claiming any original QKV idea.

## Sharp paper-level caveat

MomentKV (Yu Li, Binxu Li, Tian Lan, arXiv 2606.01563v1) states in Appendix B.2 that its correction is always at least as accurate as plain eviction. The finite-cache output counterexample in R010-C applies directly to the published equations; it does NOT show their reported Qwen3/LLaMA benchmark results are false, and it is not an inspection of an authenticated released implementation.
