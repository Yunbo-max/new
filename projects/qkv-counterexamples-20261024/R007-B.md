Retain gains, epsilon, RoPE, V, mask and output projection. Scalar reweighting commutes with RoPE. No exact-erasure assertion: positive-epsilon RMSNorm remains injective in exact arithmetic under nonzero gains. At eta1 denominator cancellation still retains gains.
Key factors vary across visible tokens, changing s_j to (r_j/r0)^eta s_j. Query-only scaling multiplies a whole content-logit row by one scalar; inverse temperature restores it exactly in real arithmetic. This is a positive control. Equal key radii also reduce to temperature. Do not scale additive biases.
## New local criterion
At eta0 let a_j=log(r_j/r0), p=softmax(s), y=sum p_j v_j.
h=dy/deta=sum p_j(v_j-y)s_j a_j.
g=dy/dalpha at alpha1=sum p_j(v_j-y)s_j.
A small temperature alpha=1+c eta leaves first-order change eta(h+c g). Minimum residual h_perp=h-g(g^T h)/||g||² for g!=0; h otherwise.
Nonzero residual rules out infinitesimal scalar cancellation at this fixed output interface. Zero does not prove finite/global equality.
One Qwen KV head serves two query heads. Apply their output-projection blocks and sum key-radial tangents into h_group. Form G=[g1,g2] from projected temperature tangents. Allowing two independent privileged temperatures leaves h_group-G G^dagger h_group. This includes cross-head cancellation, not downstream nonlinear or retraining equivalence.
