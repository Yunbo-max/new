## QKV-A: The certificate belongs to an uncertainty set, not the observed QKV data

**Interface.** Centered observed pair `(y1,D)` with `a=Cov(y1,D)`, `v=Var(D)>0`; target `theta` is not exposed at adaptation time. Candidate correction `h=y1+beta D`. Hidden `c=Cov(theta,D)` is known only through an explicitly validated domain assumption `|c|<=C`.

The true task-risk difference is `R_c(beta)-R_c(0)=v beta^2+2 beta(a-c)`. The ordinary robust solution over `c in [-C,C]` minimizes `v beta^2+2 a beta+2 C|beta|` and is `beta_rob=-sign(a)(|a|-C)_+/v`, exactly as R010 established. R011 checks its **out-of-set failure**: `(a,v,C)=(.75,2,.4)` gives `beta_rob=-.175`, a guaranteed maximum risk change `-.06125` while the calibrated assumption holds. The same observed `(y1,D)` can support a task target with `c=.8`; the true excess risk of that *unchanged correction* is `+.07875` (worse than `beta=0`). A joint Gaussian construction is feasible: let `D~N(0,2)`, `y1=.375D+noise`, `theta=.4D+other_noise`, with independent residual noises, yielding `Cov(y1,D)=.75`, `Cov(theta,D)=.8`. The task definition, not the observable distribution, changed. This is a falsification of transferring a robustness guarantee across hidden task definitions, **not** of the valid in-set minimax derivation.

**Prior-art/repair.** This is elementary distributionally robust quadratic optimization; shrinkage/ridge/GLS, abstention (`beta=0`), and held-out task labels/rewards are mandatory baselines. An algorithm cannot infer a guaranteed nontrivial `C` from unlabeled `(y1,D)` alone when different target definitions remain observationally identical. A native attention method would first need a genuinely shared task-relevant target and a held-out validation bound for every conditioned regime. Without either, call the node analytically bounded but **native-blocked**, not a new QKV algorithm.

**Next falsifier.** With known task labels or reward, fit a valid confidence bound on `Cov(theta,D|context)`, charge all labels and compute equally to ordinary ridge/WO, and test whether nonzero robust correction improves held-out NLL/accuracy over trivial `beta=0`. Failure/strong-baseline equivalence parks architectural novelty, not the mathematical history.

## QKV-A-signal-noise-identifiability: Correct Bayes is not robust to inverted labels

**Interface.** The R010 two-world Gaussian label model has hidden sign `s=+/-1`, `d_i~N(0,2)`, `r_i=s*g*d_i+zeta_i`, noise `zeta_i~N(0,sigma_eff^2)`, `g=.25,T=9,sigma_eff^2=1.47368421`. Under correct likelihood and equal prior the Bayes coefficient uses `mu=tanh(g*sum(d_i*r_i)/sigma_eff^2)` and has excess risk `2g^2 E[(mu-s)^2]`; it never exceeds the no-label midpoint's `2g^2=0.125` *when the generative assumptions are correct*.

Introduce a dataset/domain-level inversion indicator `F~Bernoulli(rho)` unknown to the estimator. In inverted domains, labels are `r_i=-s*g*d_i+zeta_i` while **the target action still depends on the true s**. The system applies the original, wrong likelihood. With `n->infinity`, its posterior converges to `mu=-s` in inverted domains and `mu=s` otherwise. Hence excess MSE converges to `8g^2*rho=0.5*rho` rather than to zero. Any `rho>1/4` eventually makes using these apparently plentiful labels **worse** than the unlabeled midpoint.

**Executed CPU test.** Rather than allocating `R*n` datasets, we sample the exact sufficient statistics `S=sum d_i^2 ~ 2 chi-square_n` and `sum d_i*r_i=+/-g*S + sqrt(sigma_eff^2*S)*Z`, with `Z~N(0,1)`. 200,000 synthetic calibration episodes per n, seed 20261024. At n=16 and rho=.3, posterior-mean excess risk `.14067`, above unlabeled `.125`; at n=256/rho=.3 it is `.149999` vs `.125`. Correct-likelihood n=256 risk is about `3.3e-7`; inverted-label n=256 is about `.5`. The shift means the nominal likelihood is false; it does not contradict R010 Bayes optimality *under its model*.

**Prior-art/repair.** Robust Bayesian inference and distribution-shift detection are established fields; the example just blocks universal 'labels always help' claims. Compare likelihood calibration, label-domain validity checks, standard robust/ridge supervision, explicit fallback and held-out target loss before any QKV-specific design. Task oracle labels and inversion flag are *not* available for free. The test is synthetic, not pretrained-Qwen evidence.
