## A parent — optimize the entire projected output, not one head in isolation

**Information and objective.** On a *fixed supervised readout*, two centered head errors are \(e_1,e_2\) and final error is \(e_1+e_2\). A controllable partial reduction of head 1 changes that to \((1-\lambda)e_1+e_2\), with \(\lambda\in[0,1]\). The oracle is allowed to inspect labeled residual covariances; a deployed QKV model does **not** automatically have them. Let \(v_1=\operatorname{Var}(e_1)>0\) and \(c=\operatorname{Cov}(e_1,e_2)\).

**Exact new derivation.** With baseline \(R_0=\mathbb E(e_1+e_2)^2\),

\[
R(\lambda)-R_0=\lambda^2v_1-2\lambda(v_1+c),\qquad
\lambda_\star=\operatorname{clip}(1+c/v_1,0,1).
\]

For \(c>-v_1\), an infinitesimal positive repair helps; for \(c\leq-v_1\), every positive \(\lambda\leq1\) is nonbeneficial (strictly harmful when \(c<-v_1\)). This is stronger than R014's *full repair* criterion \(c<-v_1/2\), since a safe **partial** repair can exist when full repair fails.

**Executed CPU fixtures.** Generate \(e_1\sim N(0,1),\eta\sim N(0,0.6^2),e_2=-c_0e_1+\eta\) with seed 20261024 and 100,000 samples per case. For \(c_0=0.8\), the theory gives unmodified MSE 0.400, optimal \(\lambda=0.2\) MSE 0.360, full repair MSE 1.000. For \(c_0=1.2\), unmodified MSE 0.400, optimal \(\lambda=0\) MSE 0.400, full repair MSE 1.800. Executed Monte Carlo approximations and assertions are in `R015_metrics.json`. No control changes the information provided to the oracle.

**Simple repair and closest prior.** This is ordinary quadratic/GLS shrinkage, not a new neural operator. R014 already identifies WitCert (arXiv:2607.28699) on real cross-layer cancellation, and OptR (arXiv:2608.02691) on post-output projection distortion. A method claiming independent head improvement must compare a **jointly** fit ordinary control under the same calibration labels and sample count.

**Discriminating native falsifier.** In pinned Qwen3-0.6B, for a fixed teacher-output or supervised loss, evaluate after \(W_O\) and after a downstream Jacobian, estimate \(c,v_1\) on calibration only, compare no change / isolated full repair / joint ridge-GLS / partial classical shrinkage on separate tasks. A future loss may be nonlinear and does not obey the fixed scalar-readout identity exactly. If ordinary joint shrinkage or zero action solves the task, no new QKV method follows. ## A child — trusted anchor cost of a robust flip-rate interval

**Information boundary.** Previous R012–R014 use a hidden domain-wide sign flip \(F\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\rho)\), and R014 assumes an *externally justified* upper bound \(\rho\leq\rho_{\max}\). Here \(m\) **independent trusted anchor domains** actually reveal the flip indicator and cost labeled supervision. Let \(K\sim\mathrm{Binomial}(m,\rho)\). A one-sided Hoeffding upper bound at confidence \(1-\delta\) is
\[
U=\min\!\left(1,{K\over m}+\sqrt{{\log(1/\delta)\over2m}}\right),\qquad
\kappa=\max(0,1-2U).
\]
The fixed action \(\kappa\) is the ordinary robust sign shrinkage from R014. Its true excess risk in the ideal effective-sign-observed setup is \(2g^2[1+\kappa^2-2\kappa(1-2\rho)]\). Conditional on valid coverage and \(\rho\leq1/2\), the action does not increase this risk against the zero-action midpoint. Failed coverage may break the guarantee; no claim of deterministic safety.

**Exact discrete CPU enumeration.** With \(g=.25,\rho=.30,\delta=.05\), enumerate all \(K=0,\ldots,m\) using exact binomial probabilities. For \(m=16\): action probability .245856, expected excess risk .123506 vs no-action .125. For \(m=64\): action .816934, expected .116769. For \(m=128\): action .988895, expected .111629. Coverage is at least .95 in each enumerated case; these are *synthetic trusted-anchor draws*, not actual domains. At the population point \(K/m=.30\), nominal \(m>37.45\) is needed merely for \(\kappa>0\), and \(m>66.57\) for \(\kappa\geq0.1\). Finite-sample random counts make these **nominal** thresholds, not a hard activation rule.

**Information caveats, prior collision and falsifier.** Trusted independent sign anchors are far stronger than the unlabeled QKV data. Domains may be correlated; multiple queries/heads require simultaneous-confidence accounting, not reusing a 95% interval as a portfolio-wide guarantee. This is textbook binomial concentration / hierarchical Bayes rather than a new attention mechanism. A practical benchmark must explicitly charge anchor count, compare standard empirical/hierarchical Bayes, calibrated ridge, abstention, and assess out-of-domain \(\rho\) shift.
