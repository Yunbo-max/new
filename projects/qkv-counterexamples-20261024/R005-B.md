## Exact operator and derivative
For q_alpha=softmax(alpha c), V in R^(n x dv), y=V^T q, target y*=V^T p, define g=y'=Cov_q(V,c). This follows from q'_j=q_j(c_j-E_q c).
Output L=||y-y*||²/2 satisfies L'=(y-y*)^T g and L''=||g||²+(y-y*)^T y''. It need not be convex. Local Gauss–Newton step -g^T(y-y*)/||g||² needs g!=0, constraint handling and actual loss check; it is not a global solution. g=0 means only first-order insensitivity.
Probability changes invisible to this output obey 1^T delta p=0 and V^T delta p=0. Use output-projected V for a fixed linear projection; downstream nonlinear equivalence is only local through a Jacobian.
## Exact counterexample to the KL oracle as strongest output control
l=(1,0,-2), c=(1,0,-1), p=(e,1,e^-2)/Z, Z=e+1+e^-2, V=(0,1,0).
With t=exp(alpha), output is 1/(t+1+t^-1). Let D=e+e^-2>2; tV=(D+sqrt(D²-4))/2 gives unique positive alphaV=log(tV), exact output error zero.
R004 KL optimum instead matches E[c]. If it also matched the middle probability, normalization and E[c] would force all probabilities equal, contradicting nonproportional centered logits. Hence alphaV!=alphaKL and the KL optimum has positive output error. Task-optimal temperature is a third objective.

Important supervisor correction: actual RMSNorm is not exact radial erasure. Write F(x)=Gamma x/sqrt(||x||²/d+epsilon). With epsilon>0, nonzero gamma entries and exact arithmetic, u=Gamma^-1 F(x) has s=||u||²<d, and ||x||²=epsilon*s/(1-s/d). Thus the map is injective. Its inverse becomes ill-conditioned near saturation. For a>0:
F(ax)=a sqrt(r²/d+epsilon)/sqrt(a²r²/d+epsilon) F(x).
Radial log sensitivity is epsilon/(r²/d+epsilon). Exact invariance occurs in the epsilon=0 idealization; finite precision or zero gains require separate analysis. Weight gains have not been downloaded/checked for zeros.
RoPE is applied after gains/normalization; preserve this ordering and GQA sharing. Removing the entire norm also removes learned gains and is a confounded intervention. Existing ideal-L2 witnesses remain true only under their stated operator assumptions.
