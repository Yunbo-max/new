# R007-A — Unlabeled variance can erase task signal
Parent QKV-A; retained R005/R006 calibration analysis. New derivation, no execution.
Let y1=theta+epsilon, corrected output y1+beta D, E epsilon=E D=0, finite second moments; calibration independent of test. Define R(beta)=E(epsilon+beta D)^2 and J(beta)=Var(y1+beta D).
Expansion gives J=R+Var(theta)+2Cov(theta,epsilon)+2 beta Cov(theta,D).
For Var(D)>0, beta_R=-Cov(epsilon,D)/Var(D), beta_J=-Cov(y1,D)/Var(D), hence beta_J-beta_R=-Cov(theta,D)/Var(D).
Objectives differ by a beta-independent constant iff Cov(theta,D)=0. Independence suffices; nonzero means require additional squared-bias accounting.
The excess risk from minimizing variance is exactly Cov(theta,D)^2/Var(D). Thus R006's independence assumption is substantive, not automatic for attention heads.
Counterexample: independent zero-mean theta,z with Var theta=T>0, Var z=1, y1=theta+z, y2=2theta+z, D=theta. Then J=(1+beta)^2 T+1 and R=1+beta²T. Variance chooses beta=-1, output z, risk T+1; risk chooses beta=0, risk1. Infinite calibration does not fix this objective error.
Cross-fitting removes fitting/evaluation reuse but not this failure: held-out variance also rewards removing signal. Shrinkage alone does not certify signal preservation.
Native audit for o_h=sum a_hj v_hj and proposed o_h+beta d_h must specify: source of d_h; common target signal; coordinate alignment; absence of future-token/label leakage; why D plausibly lacks target signal; independent task metrics. Different heads need not estimate the same signal.
Candidate probe: fixed model/readout, paired contexts whose answers are independently verified invariant, fit coefficient on separate calibration only. Measure both variance and held-out correct-answer log probability/NLL/accuracy. Answer invariance does not prove latent Cov(theta,D)=0.
