## Formal negative insight

Existing work often compares errors at one layer, one head, or one attention row. The current mathematical results show *three independent limits to that reasoning*:

- **Objective aggregation:** the sign of the change in summed task loss is not determined by signs of the per-head error changes (`QKV-A`).
- **Calibration/generalization:** the existence of a query-conditional or row-oracle repair is not a guarantee that one deployable calibration works on unseen queries (`QKV-B`).
- **Post-deletion identifiability:** a deterministic retained statistic does not generally reveal whether even a *small* step in a proposed correction direction is beneficial (`QKV-C`).

These are known linear algebra, decision-theoretic and information-theoretic phenomena in more general settings. They are not claimed as undiscovered QKV theorems or new architecture proposals. Their research value is to **disqualify spurious architectural motivations** and to define harder, real-model falsifiers.
