## A: Even infinite unlabeled calibration cannot identify task-dependent correlation
R007-A proved population variance minimization can erase true signal when Cov(theta,D)!=0. We now tighten its *observation boundary* with an indistinguishable pair of task worlds:
Let theta~N(0,T), z,u~N(0,1) independent, y1=theta+z, y2=theta+2z+u, d=y2-y1=z+u, h_beta=y1+beta*d. Calibration observes any number of unlabeled full pairs (y1,y2), no target theta labels. Define alternative valid task targets theta_s=theta+s*g*d, where s=+1 or -1, g>0. Both worlds have EXACTLY the same observable pairs, but their task risks and risk-minimizers are
 R_s(beta)=1/2+2(beta+1/2-s*g)^2 ; beta*_s=-1/2+s*g.
For ANY coefficient (possibly randomized) chosen only from those observationally identical data, the two-world average excess risk is
 1/2 sum_s E[R_s(beta)-1/2] = 2E[(beta+1/2)^2]+2g^2 >=2g^2.
This lower bound does not shrink as unlabeled n increases. It does not hold when target/reward labels identify the task, nor when independence theta ⟂ d is a valid assumption. The targets are not the same across worlds, which is exactly the identifiability boundary and NOT a general impossibility result for trained attention.
R006 unlabeled covariance estimator has E beta_hat=-1/2, Var(beta_hat)=(T+1/2)/[2(n-3)] for n>3. Its shifted-task risk becomes 1/2+(T+1/2)/(n-3)+2g². At T=9,n=24,g=1: original expected risk .9523809524, shifted risk 2.9523809524. Local CPU Monte Carlo 100000 sets per n with seed20261024 gives original n=8 risk 2.41187 versus analytic2.4; n=24 .95109 versus .95238; n=64 .65528 versus .65574; averaged +/-shift adds exactly2 for each fitted beta. Standard OLS/GLS/ridge, explicit rewards and multihead mixing remain the simple baselines. No native attention shared-target variable or visible correlation signal has been validated.

## B: A positive-epsilon Qwen3 RMSNorm is injective over reals yet collides after FP16/BF16 cast
R007-B pinned source/model and proposed radial interventions; this child studies a DISTINCT precision operator. Pinned Qwen3 implementation:
https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/5f4ecf2d9f867a1255131d2461d75793c0cf1db2/src/transformers/models/qwen3/modeling_qwen3.py
Qwen3RMSNorm first casts input to FP32, normalizes using RMS with epsilon=1e-6, casts normalized values BACK to the original input dtype, then multiplies by trained per-channel gain. It applies separately on per-head projected Q and K before RoPE, GQA/attention and V/WO output.
For nonzero gains Gamma, F(x)=Gamma*x/sqrt(u+epsilon), u=||x||²/d>0, is injective over exact reals; a radial doubling has common ratio
 F(2x)/F(x)=2sqrt(u+epsilon)/sqrt(4u+epsilon)=1+3epsilon/(8u)+O((epsilon/u)^2).
With u=1 and epsilon=1e-6, radial contrast is ~3.75e-7. CPU Torch 2.10.0+cpu executed source-order norm with UNIT gain at head_dim=128. Inputs all ones versus all twos yield BITWISE IDENTICAL postcast BF16 and FP16 tensors, but FP32 outputs differ by 3.5762786865e-7 per component. The collision happens at the explicit dtype cast even before a common trained gain. This is a conditional numerical interface witness, not a claim of a pretrained model failure: learned gains, actual activations, quantized/fused backends, V, residual, RoPE positions and downstream semantic utility were NOT tested.
Closest source collision: QKNorm and ICLR2026 QUEST https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00199, ICML2026 NaLaFormer https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/meng26g.html. ## C: MomentKV first-order correction can be WORSE than plain eviction under its own Eq9/11/12
Primary original MomentKV (arXiv:2606.01563v1) https://arxiv.org/html/2606.01563v1, §4.1/Algorithm1/Appendix B.2. Appendix B.2 explicitly states that its corrected output is ALWAYS at least as accurate as ordinary eviction. We supply exact counterexamples to that UNQUALIFIED operator statement, not a refutation of its measured average benchmark performance.

1. High-spread and reachable eviction partition. Retain token k_R=0,v_R=M=4. Evict two tokens (k,v)=(+t,+1),(-t,-1). At an earlier query orthogonal to these keys, the paper's residual-based eviction can discard both +/-1 before the larger-magnitude retained value4 (verified by a CPU implementation of the published scoring rule). Future query scores these keys at +/-t and R at0. Then:
  f_full=(M+2sinh t)/(1+2cosh t),
  f_plain=M,
  f_MomentKV=(M+2t)/3
since mean E key/value0, nE2, cross sum2t and Jensen Zhat_E=2 vs retained Z_R=1. Whenever t>M>1, the approximate output exceeds the MAXIMUM stored source value M while the exact full attention lies strictly below M; thus |f_MomentKV-f_full| > |f_plain-f_full|. At M4,t8: full1.0010058253, plain abs error2.9989941747, moment abs error5.6656608414. Jensen self-suppression fails because Zhat_E stays 2 and estimated E mixing weight remains 2/3 AS t grows; it does not shrink to zero when true Z_E=2cosh t grows. One cannot infer estimated weight->0 from Zhat_E/Z_E->0 when the retained true mass simultaneously tends to zero.

2. Small-spread exact plain baseline also harmed. Use t=1, retained v_R=tanh1=0.76159415596, same evicted keys/values. Then f_full=f_plain=v_R (zero eviction error), but Eq12 outputs (v_R+2)/3=0.92053138532 (error0.15893722936). Earlier eviction query logit5 on retained and 0 on E keeps R through the actual residual scoring sequence. Thus even modest t and reachable query switch contradict unconditional dominance.

3. Two post-eviction worlds with EXACTLY identical retained moment statistics. Let E keys k=(-1,0,+1), E values v_s=k+s*4*(1,-2,1), s in +/-1; retain key0,value2. Both worlds have (n,sumK,sumV,sumVK)=(3,0,0,2) and can evict all 3 E under previous retained logit10 vs E0, as our CPU scoring check verifies. For future query1, plain eviction output2 and MomentKV estimate1 in both. But exact full outputs are 1.70955009 (s=+) and 0.00113196 (s=-). Correction hurts the first world (error .70955 vs plain .29045) and helps the second (error .99887 vs plain 1.99887). A deterministic gate receiving ONLY identical moments/retained cache/query cannot universally know whether correction is safe.
The general missing-information principle and randomized error certificates are independently studied by Xie (arXiv:2607.21475v1) https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21475v1. RestoreKV https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01247v1 synthesizes extra states BEFORE eviction with full KV accessible. These are explicit prior-art/stronger-information collisions, not proposed brand-new mechanisms.

4. Two-token exact repair is NOT memory savings. For a 2-entry E group k=mu_k +/- delta_k, v=mu_v +/- delta_v, with x=q/sqrt(d), u=x^T delta_k, C=delta_v delta_k^T, S=delta_k delta_k^T:
 exact f_E=mu_v + [tanh(sqrt(x^T S x))/sqrt(x^T S x)] Cx,
 exact Z_E=2 exp(x^T mu_k)cosh(sqrt(x^T S x)).
The 0/0 factor has continuous limit1. Factored means+delta parameters take 2(dk+dv) numbers, SAME as two original KV pairs. Dense means+C+S at dk=dv=128 cost 33024 scalars vs 512 originals (64.5x). Thus adding second moments trivially fixes the two-token Taylor witness but is not a new compression method at equal resources.
