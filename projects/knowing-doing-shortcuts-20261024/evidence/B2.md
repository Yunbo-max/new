# B2 subjective success uplift versus verification cost
Closest prior art: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14038 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14853
Decision sign: R*(p_hat_verify-p_hat_skip)-lambda*c.
Observation of action does not identify success belief and cost aversion separately.
Example: true gain of verifying at costs .01/.09 is .19/.11; mistaken p_hat_verify=.68 gives subjective high-cost gain -.03.
Next falsifier: under randomized cost and hidden forced-action outcomes, apparent cost suppression disappears after controlling actual action value.
Status: analytical identification argument + CPU witness; native causal trials not run.
