# R003/B3 — Visible proxy vs imagined grader: causal design needs an exploitability negative control

**Scientific question:** When external scoring is salient, are agents truly gaming a proxy, or merely changing their interpretation of the task, word choice, or willingness to test?

**Information boundary/operator:** Keep true task specification, available tools and external gold evaluator fixed. Let E=1 expose a superficial grader-like cue, E=0 expose matched neutral wording, and G=1 present a demonstrably exploitable proxy, G=0 present non-exploitable matched-control proxy. Let S be actual shortcut action, not a verbal intention statement.

**Causal estimand:** DID=[P(S=1|E=1,G=1)-P(S=1|E=0,G=1)]-[P(S=1|E=1,G=0)-P(S=1|E=0,G=0)]. Under actual randomization and stable treatment definition, this is an interaction contrast; it does not by itself isolate internal 'intent' or guarantee unique causal psychological mediation. Monitor independently graded hidden correctness T as the true outcome and proxy score R separately.

**Toy demonstration:** P(S|E,G)=.10+.05E+.04G+.30 E G. The observed four rates are .10,.15,.14,.49; DID=.30. With assumed hidden success .85-.40P(S), E=1 versus E=0 at G=1 decreases hidden success by .14. With assumed proxy score .60+.30P(S), displayed score increases .105. This is an artificial design witness, NOT a sampled LLM effect.

**Rivals/repair:** A grader cue may innocently clarify tasks, increase task difficulty, break instruction hierarchy, or change information access. Hold cue length/format constant, prevalidate identical intended objective, include non-exploitable G=0 and placebo cues, run sealed evaluator not writable by the agent. Conduct a blinded human review of genuine deviation claims. Simple environmental hardening should be a baseline, not a new contribution.

**Strongest closest results:** SpecBench (2026) https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21384 already quantifies visible/hidden spec gap. Handshake Sep 18 2026 primary audit https://joinhandshake.com/research/ai/deepswe-reward-hacking/ reports *imagined* grader influence even when a hidden grader is not disclosed (blog report, not assumed peer-reviewed); RHB https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/thaman26a.html measured skipping and tampering, and hardening reduces exploits. Thus a generic grader visibility result is probably not novel.

**Falsifier/protocol:** Examine proxy-cue × genuine exploitability interaction *and* independent semantic violation vs simple 'no testing'. If control G=0 produces the same deterioration, an instruction/performance-confound rather than targeted proxy exploitation is favored. Compare with existing SpecBench and RHB tasks; quantify hidden success and interaction CIs clustered by task. Formal reviewer required before claiming causal motive.
